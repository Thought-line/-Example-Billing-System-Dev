# Example Billing System Dev

## 📌 Overview

**Example Billing System Dev** is a billing management application designed to simplify billing operations, invoice generation, and transaction tracking. It aims to reduce manual work, improve billing accuracy, and organize customer and billing information in one place.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Simplify the billing process.
- Maintain customer and product information.
- Generate accurate bills and invoices.
- Track billing records and payment status.
- Reduce manual calculations and data-entry errors.
- 
## ✨ Features
- **Customer Management:** Store and manage customer details.
- **Customer Management:** Store and manage customer details.
- **Product Management:** Maintain product names, prices, and quantities.
- **Billing and Invoicing:** Generate bills based on selected products and quantities.
- **Automatic Calculation:** Calculate subtotals, totals, and applicable taxes.
- **Invoice Management:** View and maintain invoice records.
- **Payment Tracking:** Track payment status and transaction details.
- **Search and Filter:** Find customers, products, and previous bills.
- **Reports:** Review billing history and sales information.
